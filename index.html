Burnside High School Chess Club

About Chess

Chess is a classic board game which requires strategy and critical thinking. It’s a game of endless possibilities, where every decision shapes the path to victory or defeat. Join our club to explore the rich history of the game and improve your strategic thinking.

What do we do?

We meet on Fridays in P4.

We take part in tournaments ranging from serious with lots of prizes to casual tournaments for beginners.

Achievements

We have numerous achievements as a club. Our school team placed 1st equal in the Canterbury Interschools tournament. Although only one team gets a place in the national tournament, our great performance allowed us to receive a wildcard to participate in the national tournament. We had similar success in the Interschool Individual Tournament where we had a member of our school in the podium for almost each year level from 9 - 13.

The Game Of The Century

In 1956, a 13-year-old Bobby Fischer stunned the chess world with what became known as the “Game of the Century.” Playing as Black against International Master Donald Byrne in New York, Fischer sacrificed his queen in a dazzling combination that showcased extraordinary foresight and calculation well beyond his years. The game garnered significant publicity and contributed to Fischer's rapid rise, leading to him becoming the world's youngest grandmaster and later the World Chess Champion.
